# The Bugs Were Always There: an IDSW 2026 keynote, checked (source note)

Public page: https://vanipy.com/kaji/bugs-were-always-there/ . Reading of 60 visually inspected photos of Eko Kurniawan Khannedy's IDSW 2026 keynote slides (photos received 22 Nov 2026), through the thank-you slide numbered 71. Partial record with known gaps; missing slides not reconstructed; photos not reproduced.

## Attribution status

Speaker's name printed on the closing/thank-you slides. No appearance-based identification.

## What was checked (22 Nov 2026)

- Netflix concurrency-limits official repository: Java library, congestion-control ideas, server/client limiters, excess-traffic rejection. Supports the concept only; does not prove the keynote's graphs.
- Redis sorted-set documentation: score/rank operations. Concept support only.
- NIST SP 800-38G Rev. 1 second public draft page (3 Feb 2025): FPE small-domain risks and method changes; a draft, not a certification of the talk's queue construction. Cited as caution against name-based security inference.
- The slide's cost ratio does not divide cleanly from the displayed integers (about 14,478 vs the slide's 14,400; request divisors not received), so the page does not reuse those numbers; the Sanggup cost exercise uses fresh synthetic figures. Not adopted.

## Scope decisions

- Speaker's results (zero position lookups, zero collisions at one million, 32-byte draw secret, days-to-hours proof time) attributed, not reproduced or endorsed. "Tickets cannot be forged" treated as an absolute claim to describe, not guarantee. Closing rhetoric kept as exhortation.
- Sanggup adaptation: Coding stages cd30-cd32 (race reproduction, N+1 journey count, stateless-scope mapping exercise), all synthetic and local; no traffic to third-party services; no hand-rolled crypto exercise.
- Private source-photo references live only in the collector's internal synthesis; not published.

Diagrams: the four worked-example graphics on the page (race timeline, cross-API N+1, opening-time CDN plot, ten-registrant shuffle) are original recreations drawn from the photographed slides on 23 Nov 2026, with Indonesian labels translated. Source photos remain unpublished in the private collection.
