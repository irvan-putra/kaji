- Kristin Neff, "What is Self-Compassion? (the three elements)": confirmed (self-compassion.org). Mindfulness, common humanity, self-kindness; the talk's closing arc maps onto them and credits the source.
- Steve Yegge pacing post (slide date 14 Nov 2026) and Thariq @trq212 P0 post (slide date 19 Oct 2026): slide-cited, not independently verified.
