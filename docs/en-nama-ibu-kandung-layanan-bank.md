# Mother's maiden name in bank services: digital authentication and SLIK reporting

Research note. Sources accessed 17 November 2026; verification status is stated per section.

## 1. Digital service authentication (POJK 21/POJK.03/2023)

**Verified from the regulation text.** The explanation of the authentication-factor provisions of POJK Number 21 of 2023 on Digital Services by Commercial Banks describes the knowledge factor ("something you know") as something known only to the customer and/or prospective customer, "antara lain username, password, personal identification number/PIN, nama ibu kandung, atau bentuk lain yang dapat dipersamakan" (among others username, password, PIN, mother's name, or other equivalent forms). This means the mother's maiden name is only one example of a knowledge factor; the regulation does not require the mother's maiden name as an authentication factor, and banks may use other equivalent forms.

Source: https://www.ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Documents/Pages/Layanan-Digital-oleh-Bank-Umum/POJK%2021%20Tahun%202023.%20Layanan%20Digital%20oleh%20Bank%20Umum.pdf

*Unverified:* the detailed claim that electronic verification still requires two-factor authentication and biometrics has not been checked paragraph by paragraph; what is verified is the knowledge-factor phrase above.

## 2. Debtor reporting to SLIK (POJK 11 of 2024 + SEOJK 11/SEOJK.01/2024)

**Verified from the regulation text.** The explanation of Article 4(1)(a) of POJK Number 11 of 2024 (the second amendment to POJK 18/POJK.03/2017 on Debtor Reporting and Information Requests through SLIK) states that the debtor information reported "antara lain berisi informasi mengenai nama, nomor identitas, tempat lahir, tanggal lahir, alamat, ..., nama gadis ibu kandung, informasi pasangan, pekerjaan, ..." (among others contains information on name, identity number, place of birth, date of birth, address, ..., mother's maiden name, spouse information, occupation, ...). So the mother's maiden name is indeed among the debtor data reported to SLIK. The words "antara lain" (among others) mark an illustrative list, not a statement of mandatory fields.

Source: https://www.ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Documents/Pages/Perubahan-Kedua-POJK-Nomor-18-2017-tentang-Pelaporan-dan-Permintaan-Informasi-Debitur-melalui-SLIK/POJK%2011%20Tahun%202024%20Pelaporan%20dan%20Permintaan%20Informasi%20Debitur%20Melalui%20SLIK.pdf

*Unverified:* the claim that the Annex to SEOJK 11/SEOJK.01/2024 (item 37, pp. 47-48) states the mother's maiden name column is "mandatory (cannot be left blank)". The annex PDF (286 pages) could not be retrieved from the OJK site for direct checking, so this mandatory status and the item/page numbers are unverified. SEOJK regulation page: https://ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Pages/Pelaporan-dan-Permintaan-Informasi-Debitur-Melalui-Sistem-Layanan-Informasi-Keuangan-11-SEOJK01-2024.aspx

## 3. Father's name and debtors who do not know their birth parents

- The debtor-information list in the Explanation of Article 4(1) of POJK 11/2024 is illustrative ("antara lain") and mentions the mother's maiden name without mentioning the father's name. The full list of Debtor Report fields is in the Annex to SEOJK 11/SEOJK.01/2024, which has not been verified directly, so the claims "there is no father-name column" and "the mother-name column is mandatory" are both still *unverified*.
- *Unverified / not found:* an official procedure for filling in the mother's maiden name when the debtor does not know their birth parents (for example a child raised in an orphanage). The claim that the annex provides no substitute, special code, or exception also cannot be verified without directly examining the annex.
- **Question channel (verified):** Kontak 157 is OJK's official consumer channel, with a "Pertanyaan" (Questions) service for asking about financial-services-sector products/services. Source: https://kontak157.ojk.go.id/appkpublicportal/. Whether technical questions on SLIK reporting formats are answered through this consumer channel has not been tested; it is the channel's existence and general coverage that is verified.

## Interim conclusion

- For digital services, the mother's maiden name is only one example of a knowledge factor under the verified text of POJK 21/2023; the regulation leaves room for other equivalent factors.
- For debtor reporting to SLIK, the mother's maiden name is included in the illustrative list of debtor data in the verified POJK 11/2024; its mandatory-fill status and any exceptions are unverified because the guideline annex could not be checked directly.
