# How an ad-funded coding agent pays for itself: what Freebuff says

Status: reading of Freebuff's own pages on 2026-11-16. These are the company's claims. No revenue, expense or profit disclosures establishing actual cost coverage were found on these pages; none are claimed here. Pages can change.

## What the pages say
- Advertisers pay for the free tier. Ads appear in the app labeled "Sponsored"; some agent suggestions are sponsored too, name the sponsor, and do nothing unless the user clicks Accept. Ads are chosen partly from what the user is working on, including recent messages; the company points to its privacy policy for what is used. https://freebuff.com/blog/how-is-freebuff-free (dated Nov 7, 2026)
- Free use is limited: daily hours that depend on country (the page lists up to 30 hours a day in the US, 21 in a group of countries, 12 in another group, 5 elsewhere), with each model counting at a multiplier. The page says allowances differ because advertisers pay different amounts in different places. Same source.
- Paid plans are optional and add hours and models (the page lists Starter $8, Plus $25 and Pro $60 per month). Same source.
- Advertiser side: display ads are pay per click at a rate agreed during review, with a daily budget of at least $50. Agentic campaigns, where the agent integrates a product after a developer accepts, run under an invoiced contract with a $15,000 monthly minimum. https://freebuff.com/advertisers
- Another Freebuff explainer describes free usage as 100 Freebucks a day refilled at midnight Pacific. That conflicts with the Nov 7 post, which says daily hours refill at midnight in the user's timezone. This note does not reconcile them; the explainer is undated in the text read. https://freebuff.com/perspectives/blog/explainer/freebuff-explained-the-ad-funded-coding-agent-with-no-api-bill

## Reading
The pages describe three levers together: advertiser payments, usage limits that vary by country, and paying users. Ads are not described as funding unlimited use. Whether this covers inference costs is unknown: no revenue, cost or profit data appears in these pages.

## Unknowns
Actual ad revenue per user, cost per hour of use, what share of users pay, and which of the two refill descriptions is current.
