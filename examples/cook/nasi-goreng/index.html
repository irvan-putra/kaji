Nasi goreng with telur and kerupuk ( portionportions)
Use rice that was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge. Put a wide pan or wok on medium-high heat. Cook in batches of at most 2 portions: a crowded pan steams the rice instead of frying it.

Eat hot. Critical path: the cold rice. Warm, freshly cooked rice turns to mush in the pan. Eggs can carry Salmonella, so they are cooked until the yolk is firm. This matters most for young children, older people and anyone pregnant.

Rice safety

Cooked rice can carry bacteria whose toxins survive reheating. Cool it within 1 hour, refrigerate it, use it within 24 hours, and fry it until steaming hot throughout. Do not leave fried rice out. If unsure, throw it out.

Allergens

Kecap manis contains soy (and often wheat). Egg is optional. Check kerupuk labels for prawn or fish.

Pedas warning

Four chilies per portion is hot. Taste one slice first. Keep water or teh manis nearby.
