Spaghetti aglio e olio with parsley ( portionportions)
Bring litres of water to a boil in a large pot, with g salt. Put a wide pan on the stove, off the heat. Both threads start together.
g dry spaghetti boil min, until just short of done scoop out ml of the water, then drain tip the pasta into the pan with the garlic oil; toss 1 min over medium heat, adding the water a splash at a time until glossy twirl onto warm plates; serve at once; the sauce tightens as it cools
garlic cloves slice thinly, do not mince warm in the oil on low heat 3 to 4 min, until pale gold. Take off the heat if it browns fast.
ml olive oil + chili flakes () start the oil in the cold pan add the chili to the oil with the garlic
parsley, a small handful per portion chop finely scatter on at plating
Eat hot. Critical path: the pasta pot and the garlic, which burns in seconds if the heat is too high. Burnt garlic is bitter: if it goes dark brown, start the oil over.

Pot size

This needs a large pot: 3 litres of water or more. Use two pots if yours is smaller, and keep the pan wide so the pasta tosses freely.

Large batch

Three or four portions need a very large pan or a wok to toss in, or toss in the empty pasta pot. Otherwise cook in two batches.

Allergens

Spaghetti contains wheat (gluten). Check the label for other traces.
