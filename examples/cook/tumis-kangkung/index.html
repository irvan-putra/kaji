Eat hot. Critical path: high heat, and cooking it through. A weak flame stews the greens in their own water. Never eat kangkung raw or half-cooked. Cook in batches of at most 2 portions.

Washing

Water spinach grows in or near water and can carry germs and parasites. Washing removes grit but does not make it safe by itself: cook it until the stems are tender and everything is steaming hot, and never eat it raw.

Allergens

Oyster sauce contains shellfish and usually soy and wheat. For a vegetarian version, use a mushroom-based sauce.

Pedas warning

Four chilies per portion is hot. Taste one slice first.

Water spinach grown in contaminated water has been found to carry bacteria and parasites (example study, Cambodia), so this recipe cooks it through. General guidance: WHO.
