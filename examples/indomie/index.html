Indomie Mi Goreng: the customizable recipe box

A recipe box you can retune. Drag any blue number left or right, or tap a blue flag to flip it, and the whole card rewrites itself. Read left to right: the cooking threads run in parallel and merge as steps come together. On phones, swipe the card sideways to follow the threads.

fried shallots sachet ( packpacks) do not cook sprinkle last, straight from the pack
Eat hot, yolk broken over the noodles. Critical path: the 3:00 boil - past it the noodles ship soggy (no patch, remake). Pedas: 0 = no chili · ½ pack = level 3 · full pack = level 5 (segfault).

Pedas warning

Level exceeds the recommended envelope for most hardware. Keep teh manis within reach. Level 5 is a full chili pack per pack: segfault territory.

Scaling note

Scaling to packs is linear in noodles but not in happiness. Past four packs, reviewer attention drops sharply.
