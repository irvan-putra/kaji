YukYuk: AI media tools, credits and visibility choices

YukYuk is an Indonesian-facing creative studio that brings third-party image, video and character-generation models into one interface, with a public gallery and remixing. It is not a claim that every model is made by YukYuk, or that one privacy setting overrides all providers' processing.

What is available, and what is announced

The public app describes text-to-image, image editing/reference workflows, text-to-video and image-to-video. Model/tool/region availability can vary. Music, voice and sound effects are also advertised in company materials. No generation was run in this review, so outputs, quality and model performance were not tested.

Microdrama Studio is marked coming soon. Treat the write/cast/direct/publish concept as an announcement rather than an available end-to-end production workflow.

Pricing: keep the two pages separate

The signed-out payment page offers 50 credits for Rp50,000, then 110 for Rp100,000, 225 for Rp200,000 and 460 for Rp400,000. The marketing homepage instead says packs start at 100 credits for Rp100,000. The Rp50,000 option is present on the payment page, not merely an unverified event offer.

The free offer is 10 credits after email verification, with no card required according to the site. No signup was made. Credits are usage units, not a fixed number of finished videos: cost depends on the tool/model and settings. No per-result budget or successful output is promised here.

Expiry is unresolved: the homepage says no expiry while app help says top-up credits last at most one year. Do not buy relying on perpetual validity without confirmation. Current subscription prices were not established from the signed-out pages. Watch Premium's Rp49,000 monthly viewing offer is not a generation subscription.

Privacy Mode is not no processing

  • Paid plans or paid top-ups unlock Privacy Mode according to the payment/help pages. Also advertised: additional tools/models and watermark removal.
  • Help says prompts and uploads are shared with the relevant third-party model providers. Private visibility within YukYuk is not a promise that those providers do not process the input.
  • Public media can be remixed. Help says remixes can remain public even after the original is made private.
  • Making previously public content private does not undo earlier indexing, copying or exposure. Choose visibility before generation or publishing, especially for client work.
  • Do not upload confidential, personal or third-party material merely because the interface calls a mode private. Check consent, rights, provider terms and the actual setting.

Ownership, refunds and quality

The terms describe user ownership of content together with platform rights needed to operate/display the service. That is not a guarantee that generated material is copyrightable, exclusive or clear of third-party rights. You still need the rights to inputs and an appropriate review before commercial use.

Purchases are described as non-refundable. Automatic credit returns for technical generation failure are different from dissatisfaction with an output's quality. Confirm the applicable current policy before a purchase; no payment or refund flow was tested.

A useful first evaluation, if separately approved

Use an invented prompt with no faces, brand assets or private input. Compare the actual cost, output and visibility of one task across models, then check download rights and failed-job credit behavior. A large model list, usage counter or vendor privacy statement is not an independent quality or security test.
