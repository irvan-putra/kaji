Kubeletto: container hosting, with beta billing questions

Kubeletto describes a container platform for deploying from GitHub or OCI container images, with automatic HTTPS, revisions, autoscaling and scale-to-zero. It sits closer to managed application hosting than a static GitHub Pages site.

Do not read beta as free forever. The homepage says beta usage is visible but not yet billed and limits apply. A separate pricing page publishes a 30-day trial and a paid Pro plan. Both statements need to stay visible until the actual activation/checkout state settles what applies.

Published plan snapshot

Trial

Pricing advertises 30 days, one app, 0.5 vCPU and 512 MiB; 20 deployments, 200 build minutes and 10 GiB are listed limits. A trial is not unlimited permanent hosting, and quotas are not a performance benchmark.

Pro

The pricing page lists Rp49,000/month, five apps, 1 vCPU and 1 GiB; 500 deployments, 1000 build minutes and 100 GiB. It describes QRIS prepaid payment without auto-renewal. Checkout fees are excluded; review the current total and non-refund terms before buying.

What the platform handles, and what remains yours

The vendor advertises build/deploy flow, image-based apps, HTTPS, revisions and scaling. You still need a suitable application/container, secrets management, health checks, resource planning, data storage/backups and an understanding of network/egress costs. A scale-to-zero claim does not by itself establish cold-start latency, persistent storage, database availability or compatibility with a particular app.

Its acceptable-use page describes shared multi-tenant resources. Do not infer a dedicated machine, guaranteed CPU isolation or unlimited build work. Whether an app needs a database, jobs, long-lived connections or filesystem persistence should be checked against the current service limits.

Compare hosting categories

  • GitHub Pages: static content, not a backend/container runtime.
  • Managed app/container platforms: Kubeletto and NEO App are in a broadly related category, but their resource models, supported workloads, pricing and operational terms differ.
  • Raw VPS: more system control and more responsibility; not interchangeable with a managed deployment UI.

Kaji's cloud and email explainer explains why app hosting and managed email hosting are different purchases. A generic comparison is not a claim that any account can migrate or resell service.

Privacy and unresolved checks

The privacy policy lists retention of runtime/request logs for 8 days, build logs 14 days and audit logs 365 days. It also describes analytics and possible masked console capture. Avoid secrets or personal data in logs. These are published policy descriptions, not verified deletion behavior.

Corporate jurisdiction, exact data-center region, independent isolation/performance evidence and the applicable beta-versus-paid activation state were not established in this review. Linked repository destinations did not establish a usable public source repository, so this page does not call the product open source.

Before a deployment

  1. Resolve the actual plan, current billing state, quotas, checkout fees and cancellation/refund rules.
  2. Check runtime/container support, databases/persistence, cold starts, domains and egress for the actual workload.
  3. Use a disposable synthetic app only after separate deployment approval. Do not migrate production credentials or customer data as a trial.
