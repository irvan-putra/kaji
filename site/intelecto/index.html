Intelecto: learning with AI inside, external AI access unconfirmed

Intelecto is Erlangga's education ecosystem combining SD/MI textbooks, a school LMS and Intelecto Reader. Its public site is aimed at students, teachers and parents. It advertises AI features inside that ecosystem, which is different from offering an API for an outside AI to use.

Short answer: human-oriented and AI-assisted. External AI access, an official machine integration and permission to automate an account were not established from the public sources reviewed.

The book, the reader and the LMS

Textbooks

The homepage shows Indonesian elementary SD/MI books, including Mathematics, IPAS and Bahasa Indonesia examples for grades 3–6. It describes alignment with Kurikulum Merdeka and CP 046. These are publisher descriptions, not an independent curriculum evaluation.

Intelecto Reader

The site describes scanning Intelecto books to open interactive learning media and games. A book-linked reader is not the same as a freely downloadable corpus or a license to copy book content into another system.

School LMS

The publisher describes connecting teachers, students and parents, with a single integrated account across phone, tablet and desktop. A public LMS login form is visible, but account eligibility, school access, purchase requirements and behind-login features were not verified.

AI features

The homepage advertises AI-generated learning questions and a chatbot. It does not establish which user role gets each feature, which model is used, its current limits or whether generated questions receive teacher review. No questions or chat messages were generated.

Four different meanings of "AI can use it"

  1. AI exists inside the product: advertised on the homepage.
  2. An AI can read public product information: the public homepage was readable for this review. That says nothing about access to protected learning content.
  3. An outside program has an official integration: no public API/developer or AI-integration route was established in this review. A search miss is not proof none exists.
  4. An outside AI may operate a user's account: permission, account rules and the relevant data/content rights remain unverified. A login page alone does not answer them.

What to check before an integration

Ask the publisher or the school administrator whether an official API/integration is offered, which content and account roles it covers, and whether any account automation is permitted. Review student/child-data handling, content licensing, model-provider processing and required human review before considering a connection.

Being able to render a page or control a browser does not establish those permissions. No conclusion that automation is either permitted or universally prohibited is drawn here. No login, account creation, book scanning, private content retrieval, automation, upload or payment was done.

Sources
