From post-its to a readable noticeboard

A cleaned-up reading of community notices photographed at IDSW 2026. Repeated photos are combined; readable topics are preserved. This is an independent Kaji summary, not an official IDSW job board.

Read the labels. A verified destination means the linked site matched the topic. It does not prove a job is open, a promotion is available, or a service is safe. Claims on handwritten notes are separated from public-source checks made 21 November 2026.

Original photos, faces, personal resumes, personal profile links, email addresses and social handles are not reproduced. Personal availability notes are anonymous. Nothing was applied for, purchased, redeemed, installed or deployed.
