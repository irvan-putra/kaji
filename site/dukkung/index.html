Dukkung: one creator page for tips, sales and memberships

Dukkung presents a Rupiah-first creator platform: a shareable page for tips, a shop, paid posts, membership tiers, community features and livestream overlays. It is a platform to receive support and sell work, not simply a payment gateway.

How the model fits together

Tips and stream interaction

Supporters choose a suggested or custom amount and can leave a message. The site advertises QRIS, e-wallets and bank payment routes, with overlays for notifications, media queues and other stream interactions. Illustrative tip notifications are not verified income or a sign-up bonus.

A small storefront

The site advertises digital downloads delivered after payment and physical-product variants/address collection. Presets, ZIPs and PDFs are examples, not an assurance that every product or file type is allowed. Creators remain responsible for rights, descriptions and fulfillment.

Posts and memberships

The product describes public, follower, member and one-off-paid access to posts, plus monthly membership tiers with creator-defined benefits. Do not infer automatic recurring-billing mechanics or guaranteed renewal from the monthly price display alone.

Community and reporting

The site advertises roles, missions, targets, leaderboards and income reports. These public descriptions and sample screens were inspected, not live creator dashboards or actual payout performance.

Fees: free to open does not mean free payments

The homepage says no opening/monthly fee and advertises payment-network fees of 0.7–2%, with payout arrival 5–30 minutes. Treat those as headline vendor claims, not an exact checkout total or a guaranteed bank settlement time.

The creator terms say funds enter a creator balance after payment-gateway fees, and settled funds can be withdrawn to a verified Indonesian bank account or e-wallet subject to the payout/fee schedule. Exact method-specific fees, taxes, holds, minimums and final payout eligibility need confirmation for the actual transaction. No payment or withdrawal was tested.

Obligations and rights

  • Creator terms make creators responsible for prices, promised benefits and fulfillment. A membership benefit is an obligation, not free revenue.
  • The general terms say tips are voluntary support, not investments, loans, equity or guaranteed returns. Product sales and membership benefits carry different expectations.
  • The terms say original-content ownership remains with the creator, while the platform receives a non-exclusive worldwide royalty-free license for operating the service. Read the actual scope before uploading.
  • Accounts or funds may be restricted for policy violations, suspicious payments or legal orders. A visible balance is not unconditional cash availability.
  • The refund policy says tips are final; digital-content technical/description issues can be reported within 48 hours, and damaged physical goods require unboxing evidence within three working days. These are vendor policy terms, not a blanket refund guarantee.

Data and account review

The privacy policy describes account/contact information, transaction information and payout identifiers, potentially including tax/identity details when required. It names payment partners and other service providers, and says financial transaction records are kept at least five years. Its encryption/security statements are vendor assertions, not an audit performed here.

The site says supporters can pay without creating a permanent account. That does not mean no payment or personal data is processed. Creators have an account and verification responsibilities. No signup, identity upload, account connection, storefront, payment or transfer was made for this review.

Compare the category before building

A payment gateway processes a merchant's checkout; a creator platform bundles the public page, content access, store, supporter interaction and balance workflow around payments. A crowdfunding landing page can show a goal and backers without implementing those services. Compare the actual product, not only a donate button.
